DopeUFO - Turn-Based Tactical Game Demo

How to Play

  • Select a soldier: Click on one of the blue soldiers (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, or Delta)
  • Move: Click "Move Mode" button, then click an adjacent tile to move (costs 4 AP)
  • Shoot: Click "Shoot Mode" button, then click an enemy to attack (costs 8 AP)
  • End Turn: Click "End Turn" to let the enemies take their turn
  • Goal: Eliminate all three red Sectoid aliens!

Tips:

  • Each soldier has 20 Action Points per turn
  • Gray blocks provide cover, reducing enemy accuracy by 20%
  • Accuracy decreases with distance (5% per tile)
  • Check the UI panel on the right for unit stats and the message log
